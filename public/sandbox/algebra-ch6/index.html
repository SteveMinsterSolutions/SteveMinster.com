Quick reminders

  • Multiply or divide by a negative? Flip the sign.
  • AND = the overlap in between. OR = two pieces pointing outward.
  • Isolate the absolute value first. |x| < a → AND, |x| > a → OR.
  • Solid line for ≤ or ≥, dashed for < or >. Shade above for greater than, below for less than.
  • "Within 5 of 40" → |x − 40| ≤ 5
Tip: work each problem on paper before you tap an answer.
